# Regain's privacy policy

*Last updated: 24 September 2026. Data controller: EL IDRISSI AZAMI SAAD,
contact: saadelidrissiazami@gmail.com.*

## What Regain stores

Everything is attached to your account, identified by your email address.

| Data | What it is for | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Email address and password | creating and protecting your account | yes |
| Goals, budget, usual energy, times you are free | building you a realistic plan | yes |
| Activities planned and ticked off, energy check-ins | tracking your progress, adapting what is suggested | yes |
| Wellbeing sessions finished, how they felt (1 to 5), written answers and free notes | letting you read yourself back in your journal, showing your trends | no, you can leave all of it blank |
| Fitness profile: sex, year of birth, height, weight, activity level, equipment, diet, allergies, health notes | working out your calorie needs and adapting sessions and meals | no, only if you use the fitness coach |
| Weekly check-ins: sessions done, energy, weight | adjusting the following week's programme | no |

Weight, allergies and health information are **sensitive data**. They are only stored if you fill
in the fitness questionnaire, are used solely to personalise your programme, and are never used
for anything else.

## What Regain does not store

- **No advertising trackers, no ad network, no selling of data.**
- **No usage analytics** sent to a third party.
- **Your calendar**: syncing writes into your phone's calendar. Nothing it contains is read or
  sent to our servers.
- **Your notifications**: reminders are created and kept on your phone.

## Your location

If you ask for a walking route or the history of your neighbourhood, your location is read at that
moment and sent, without your identity, to:

- **routing.openstreetmap.de** (FOSSGIS) to draw the route;
- **nominatim.openstreetmap.org** to find the name of the area;
- **en.wikipedia.org** for its history.

Your location is not stored on our servers. Permission is asked for at the moment of use and can
be withdrawn in your iPhone's settings.

## Your coach (Premium)

If you write to your coach, **your message is sent to Anthropic** to produce the reply, along with
your last ten messages and the goals you have set. Nothing else is passed on: not your weight, not
your allergies, not your health notes — unless, of course, you write them into the message
yourself.

Your exchanges are stored in your account so you can find them again, and are erased with it.
Anthropic does not use them to train its models.

The coach is not a health professional and does not diagnose anything. The rest of the app — the
plan, the sessions, the programme, the meals, the shopping list — is worked out with no AI at all,
on your phone.

## Hosting and processors

| Service | Role | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | database and accounts | European Union (Frankfurt) |
| Apple (App Store) | subscription payments | per Apple |
| RevenueCat | tracking the state of your subscription | United States, standard contractual clauses |
| Anthropic | your coach's replies, if you use it | United States, standard contractual clauses |

Apple never passes us your card number. RevenueCat receives only your Regain account identifier
and the state of your subscription. If you installed Regain from an Apple Ads advert, Apple gives
RevenueCat an anonymous token saying which campaign it was — no tracking, no advertising identifier.

## How long it is kept

Your data stays for as long as your account exists. If you delete it, everything is erased
immediately, including your journal, your notes and your fitness profile.

## Your rights

From the app, in the **Profile** tab:

- **Export my data**: a file containing everything we hold about you;
- **Delete my account**: immediate and permanent erasure.

You can also write to saadelidrissiazami@gmail.com to access your data, correct it, object to a
particular processing, or lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL
([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)). If you are in another EU or EEA country, you may complain to
your own national authority instead.

## Children

Regain is for adults. The fitness coach requires a year of birth corresponding to 18 or over.

## Changes

If anything significant changes, the date at the top of this page is updated and the app says so
the next time it starts.
